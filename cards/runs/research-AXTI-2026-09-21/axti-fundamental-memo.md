## Value chain and competitive economics
AXT grows compound-semiconductor crystals and finishes bare substrates; it does not perform epitaxy or capture all downstream laser/module economics. Indium/phosphorus → crystal growth/VGF → substrate wafer → epitaxy/laser/photodetector → transceiver/optical system → AI/datacenter. Customers include laser and module makers; announced Lumentum/Coherent relationships support exposure, not proof every hyperscaler spend dollar becomes AXT revenue.

Own furnaces, repurposed GaAs cleanroom space, low-defect larger wafers, customer qualification and raw-material joint ventures help speed delivery and capture pricing. Most customers qualify at least two suppliers. Three-inch remains volume core; four-inch migration is real, six-inch qualification is opportunity rather than fully realized revenue. China manufacturing enables cost/supply control and domestic permit-free sales, while cross-border permits can delay cash conversion.

JX primary June16 release plans up toJPY120bn incremental overfouryears, total150bn with earlier plan, InP capacity7–10x. It is announced capacity, not qualified output. Sumitomo/JX are diversified businesses; COHR/LITE downstream integrated photonics; AAOI modules. No honest pureplay peer median was established.
