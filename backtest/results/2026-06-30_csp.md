# Result — CSP on QQQ / SPY (VRP + entry timing)

**Date:** 2026-06-30  **Script:** `backtest/exp_csp.py`  **Tag:** 🟡 provisional (cost-sensitive)

## Method
20Δ short put, 30 DTE (~21 td), 50% PT, no SL, single-leg (no wheel), $50k/underlying,
BSM priced with VIX/VXN, cost 1.5%/side of premium. Three entry timings: unconditional,
low IV-rank (≤25% of trailing 252d), RSI(2)<10 dip. NAV excludes collateral T-bill interest.

## Conclusions
1. **Engine validates** — 94–97% WR, PF 1.7–3.4, matches distillation's QQQ 20Δ ~98.6% WR ballpark.
2. **CSP alpha is NOT significant** (|t| < 1.8 everywhere, often ~0/negative). The 94% WR
   and large $ P/L are mostly **BETA** (short put ≈ +0.2 delta = paid for equity downside),
   not a free VRP alpha. → "98% win rate" ≠ edge; net of realistic costs the VRP alpha is thin.
   CSP ≈ a capital-efficient ~20%-beta income sleeve, not a market inefficiency.
3. **RSI-2-timed entry is the standout** — fewer/higher-quality trades, best PF (2.9–3.4),
   lowest AvgDD (−0.8%), SPY alpha closest to significant (t 1.8). Selling puts INTO dips
   stacks the entry alpha. **Sell on RSI-2 dips, not unconditionally.**
4. **low-IV-rank (56/57) does NOT robustly replicate** for naked CSP (helped QQQ, hurt SPY) —
   discrepancy vs the bull-put-SPREAD test; needs follow-up.

## Caveats
The "VRP alpha insignificant" conclusion is **cost-sensitive** (1.5%/side assumed) — halving
costs may turn it significant. VIX/VXN as IV; single-leg (no wheel); AvgDD tiny but MaxDD
−13% to −24% (assignment in crashes is the tail). NEXT: cost-sensitivity sweep; wheel variant.
