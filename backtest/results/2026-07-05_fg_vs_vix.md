# Result — CNN Fear&Greed vs VIX: does F&G add over VIX? (complete battery)

**Date:** 2026-07-05  **Script:** `backtest/experiments/exp_fg_battery.py`
**Window:** 2016-01-01 → present  **Tag:** active ✅ — settles the one thing the fear/greed
cluster (`2026-07-03_topdays_exposure.md`) left underpowered: the incremental value of CNN F&G.

## Question
The 2026-07-03 fear/greed cluster used F&G only inside a combined signal on SPY/QQQ/SPMO,
2011+, with the F&G-specific cut at 4-6 trades. Held to the RSI-2 standard (capital-efficiency,
2016+, two-halves, 4 categories), does CNN F&G add anything over VIX?

## Method
RSI-2 dip entries (entry<10, exit RSI2>{70,80,90,95}) per instrument, classified by F&G at
entry, across 4 categories (大盤指數/細價股/板塊ETF/Mag7). F&G low=fear (inverse of VIX).
CNN F&G history via github whit3rabbit/fear-greed-data. Trade-level, look-ahead-safe,
2bp/trade, raw close. Plus a head-to-head vs VIX (pooled) and a market-level greed de-risk test.

## Result 1 — head-to-head: VIX is a strictly BETTER fear/entry gauge
Pooled RSI-2 dip trades, per-trade return% / win% (by entry fear-gauge):
| 恐懼定義 | >70 | >80 | >90 | >95 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| F&G<25 | +0.1/66 | +0.6/69 | +1.0/67 | +2.4/73 |
| F&G<15 (extreme) | +0.1/66 | +1.2/72 | +1.8/69 | +3.9/79 |
| **VIX>28** | **+1.0/71** | **+2.4/77** | **+3.5/80** | **+5.2/87** |
VIX>28 dips bounce ~2× more than F&G<25 dips at every exit, and beat even F&G<15. → F&G does
NOT add over VIX for entry timing. Mechanism: VIX directly prices option-implied fear (sharp,
forward-looking); F&G is a slow composite (momentum/breadth/put-call) that can read <25 in a
slow grind-down that is not a sharp capitulation, so the bounce is weaker.

## Result 2 — greed de-risk: this is where F&G IS uniquely useful (MONOTONIC)
SPY forward-63d return (2016+), by regime:
| regime | fwd-63d mean | win% | n |
|---|---|---|---|
| baseline | +3.5% | 77% | 2576 |
| F&G>70 | +1.1% | 68% | 439 |
| **F&G>75** | **+0.2%** | 60% | 236 |
| **F&G>80** | **−1.6%** | **54%** | 111 |
| VIX<13 | +1.7% | 74% | 499 |
| VIX<15 | +1.8% | 74% | 928 |
Forward reward decays MONOTONICALLY as greed rises (+1.1 → +0.2 → −1.6%), and turns negative
by F&G>80 — while VIX<13/<15 shows NO decay (+1.7/1.8%, ≈baseline). Monotonic + F&G-specific +
decent samples ⇒ this is a robust signal, not a single cherry-picked threshold. **Actionable
read (user):** **F&G>75 = forward reward essentially exhausted (~0) → a portfolio-level
"stop adding / trim" line; F&G>80 = reward turns NEGATIVE → stronger de-risk.** It is a
below-average-forward (not crash) signal, market-level; individual-position exit stays RSI-2
recovery. F&G's composite (breadth stretch, put/call complacency, momentum extension) captures
euphoria that low-VIX cannot.

## Result 3 — F&G-zone matrix is messy + regime-dependent (weaker than VIX)
Per-category F&G-zone matrix (PART 1): the F&G "恐懼<25" zone is NOT a reliable jackpot —
細價股 is NEGATIVE at tight/mid exits (−1.0 to −1.5%), 板塊ETF ≈0; only 大盤/Mag7 show fear-zone
strength at loose exits, and far less cleanly than VIX's extreme zone. Two-halves (PART 3,
F&G fear zone pooled): 前2016-20 NEGATIVE (−1.5 to −0.3% at tight/mid), 後2021-26 positive —
same regime-dependence as everything else, and weaker than VIX.

## Conclusions (settle)
**CNN F&G = a GREED/exit gauge, NOT a fear/entry gauge.**
- **Entry timing → use VIX, not F&G.** VIX>28 dips bounce ~2× F&G<25; the F&G-zone entry matrix
  is messy (small-caps even negative) and regime-dependent.
- **De-risking on froth → use F&G (>75 trim / >80 stronger).** Monotonic forward decay
  (+1.1 → +0.2 → −1.6% at F&G>70/75/80) where VIX<13 sees nothing (+1.7%).
This confirms, under the full battery (2016+, 4 categories, head-to-head, two-halves), the
same "VIX=fear gauge / F&G=greed gauge, each on its own side" split found earlier in-session.

## Caveats
- F&G>80 de-risk = 111 days (2016+) → direction strong, sample small. F&G = community CSV
  (whit3rabbit), 2011+, may not byte-match CNN's changing methodology.
- Greed de-risk is market-level (SPY fwd-63d), not per-category. Extreme-fear F&G buckets thin
  per category. 1bp-adjacent (2bp/trade), raw close.

## Implication for Karst
Wire F&G ONLY on the greed/exit side (a portfolio-level de-risk flag at F&G>80), not as a fear
entry signal — the entry timing belongs to VIX/RSI-2 (see `2026-07-05_rsi2_capital_efficiency.md`).
Supersedes the fear/greed cluster's implicit "F&G as part of a combined fear entry" (that cut
was 4-6 trades and, tested cleanly, loses to VIX).
