- **客戶集中度**:查不到 10-K/10-Q 中列明前幾大客戶佔收入百分比的表格(不同於 FN,AGX 文件未見「Significant customers」披露段落)。

## 5. 市場現在最擔心哪一項

- **標題**:"Is Argan (AGX) Undervalued After Its Sector Led Selloff?"——Yahoo Finance / Simply Wall St narrative,2026-08-29。原文提及「investors should weigh Argan's heavy exposure to large natural gas projects, as well as the earnings hit that could follow any major project delay or cancellation」——市場擔心單一大型燃氣電廠項目的延誤或取消風險(集中度風險),而非增長本身。
