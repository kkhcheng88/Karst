## 3. 市場敘事(報道原話)

Jefferies 是「SaaSpocalypse」一詞的命名者:24/7 Wall St.(2026-02-05)寫「Jeffries [原文如此] has "coined the mass selloff as the 'SaaSpocalypse'"」,並指 AI 令 SaaS 的功能「easily replicated and replaced by more comprehensive computing power」。
來源:https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/02/05/the-saaspocalypse-has-cut-these-stocks-in-half/

TechCrunch(2026-03-01,https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/01/saas-in-saas-out-heres-whats-driving-the-saaspocalypse/)的表述:

> "The barriers to entry for creating software are so low now thanks to coding agents, that the build versus buy decision is shifting toward build in so many cases."

同文並稱,這是「the first time in history that the terminal value of software is being fundamentally questioned」。

一句話總結(我的歸納,非引文):AI agent 能直接替人做工,按人頭收費(per-seat)的 SaaS 訂閱模式因此被質疑終值歸零;自建取代採購,軟件商由收租者變成被取代者。

  原話:
  > "But if the view is that new AI companies are going to disrupt the entire software industry, why are stocks also down that should benefit from the disruption—like the hyperscalers that invested early in AI technology... And therein lies the broken logic. The market is selling indiscriminately."

  論點:如果真信 AI 顛覆軟件,那麼顛覆的受益者(早投 AI 的超大型雲廠)不應該一齊跌;一齊跌即證明這是無差別拋售,不是有邏輯的重新定價。文中另指 S&P 500 軟件指數 RSI 跌至 18,為 1990 年以來最超賣;並稱採用 AI 的企業毛利率較未採用者高出 2–3 個百分點。點名公司:Anthropic、DeepSeek、Meta、Amazon、Alphabet、Microsoft。
