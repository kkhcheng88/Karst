# 《Stock Market Wizards》(Schwager, 2001)蒸餾

> 全書 14 位股票操作員 + 一位交易心理醫生 + Schwager 自撰的 65 條「Wizard Lessons」。本檔只抽與用戶四類注最相關的五位,加總結章。頁碼取自純文字檔 `=== PAGE n ===`。英文引述保留原文(OCR 有錯字,照抄未改)。
>
> 這本書的時代背景要先講清楚:訪問做於 1999–2000 年,即科網泡沫的頂,而且是在頂之前完成的。書裡對「有夢無盈利的概念股」的判斷,是在派對散場前寫的,不是事後孔明——這反而令它對用戶三類注/四類注特別有參考價值。

## ① 每位受訪者

### 一、Michael Lauer(Lancer)——一類注最完整的一套判準

**他是誰**:前 Oppenheimer/Kidder 科技分析員,連續七年 Institutional Investor 全明星。1993 年起自營,七年多年均複合淨回報 72%(同期羅素 2000 為 13%),最大回撤僅 8.7%。長倉集中在最多 15 隻。

**核心信念**:「這一行不是投資於偉大的公司,是從錯價的股票上獲利。」基本面沒有絕對的好壞,只有相對於價格的好壞。

1. **他要的不是相對弱勢,是災難。**「I'm not talking about relative weakness; I'm talking about a catastrophic plunge. […] when a stock goes from nearly $50 to $15 at the same time the market has been moving sharply higher, you have a debacle, not relative weakness.」(p.29)
   → **①錯殺注的第一道閘,本書對用戶最有用的一句。** 慢慢陰跌的不要,只要在大市上升時急插逾五成的。理由:急插代表有人在不計價清貨;陰跌代表基本面在逐步惡化,即「便宜有理」。
2. **賣家為什麼賣:職業風險,與生意無關。**「What gives you the opportunity is a few of the major funds deciding that owning the stock is so stigmatizing that they don't want to be associated with it. They would prefer to blow out of their position at almost any price rather than have to answer for having the stock in their portfolio at the end of the month.」(p.29)
   → **①,直接答「賣家的理由是否與生意無關」。** 這是登記表丙族「被迫賣出事件庫」在 2001 年就有的原始描述,而且指出了一種登記表未收的被迫賣:**季末窗飾式的名聲性拋售**。
3. **盈利可以妥協,資產負債表不能。**「I will compromise on disappointing reported earnings—that's usually why the stock got blasted in the first place—but I won't compromise on the balance sheet. The debt has to be manageable relative to the cash flow.」(p.29)
   → **①的第二道閘,同時是④的生存性檢查。** 盈利差是股價已跌的原因(可接受);資產負債表差是公司會死的原因(不可接受)。這與 KARST-166 的結論(四類注不能用「不燒錢」篩,只能用「不欠債」篩)完全同構。
4. **內部人買入 / 公司回購 = 安全網。**「When the senior executives are buying shares for themselves, and particularly when the company is repurchasing its stock for the treasury, it sends a strong message that the downside is often limited […] What safety net does one have buying AOL? The stock could go down 90 percent and still be only fairly priced.」(p.29)
   → **①,亦是③的紅線。** 支持登記表甲族「內部人機會性群體買入」。
5. **前五項全是防守,第六項才是賺錢。**「All five of the factors we discussed so far are defensive in nature […] They are designed to diminish risk but do not automatically translate into a significant money-making opportunity. […] The key question is: What is going to make the stock go up? It is our task to identify and time that catalyst.」(p.30)
   → **①,答「市場何時停止賣」。** 沒有催化劑的低估股就是佔死資金,這是「便宜有理」的實際代價。
6. **越絕望,越少東西就能翻身。**「the more severely depressed the valuation and the more pessimism that surrounds the stock, the less it actually takes to reverse the market's perception and trigger a price recovery.」(p.30)他的一類注目標是十二個月翻一倍——遠高於用戶對一類注 10–20% 的估計。
7. **集中是超額回報的前提。**「The greater the number of stocks you hold, the more marketlike your performance becomes […] Those who preach diversification as a risk control measure are essentially hedging their fundamental ignorance of their own holdings.」(p.24)
8. **令行業興奮的東西,同時令它變成毀滅性的投資。**「the same factors that make an industry exciting also make it a potentially ruinous investment because it will attract excess financial and intellectual capital.」(p.27)
   → **③④的紅線。** 熱敘事會吸引過量資本,把回報攤薄——這對用戶「注內集中押一兩隻」是一個反向提醒:同一敘事下的公司數目本身就是敘事已熱的指標。
9. **長短倉的風控不對稱。**「on the long side, we never use stops […] On the short side, however, because the risk is theoretically unlimited, we will use stops.」(p.31)長倉的安全網來自估值與資產負債表,不是止蝕位。
10. **1987 年的槓桿教訓**:他持有的全是便宜股,選股沒錯,但因孖展被逼平倉而錯過復元。「a lifelong aversion to leverage.」(p.32)

### 二、Dana Galante(Miramar,百分百做空)——三類注/四類注的紅線清單

**她是誰**:三藩市純空頭經理,1994–99 年均複合 15%,同期納斯達克年均升 32%。50–60 隻分散持倉,單一上限約 3%。

**核心信念**:好公司可以是壞股票;高估值本身**不足以**做空,一定要有催化劑,而且絕不空一隻正在直線上升的股。

11. **獵物剖面的三個必要條件。**「I look for growth companies that are overvalued—stocks with high P/E ratios—but that by itself is not enough. There also has to be a catalyst. […] I won't short a stock that is moving straight up. The stock has to show signs of weakening or at least stalling.」(p.52)
    → **②③的離場條件反寫。** 反過來讀:只要升勢未停,估值高本身不是離場理由。這一句對用戶二類注的持有紀律價值極高。
12. **催化劑的實際樣貌。**「companies with slowing revenue growth who have kept their earnings looking good by cutting expenses. […] Another thing I look for is a company that is doing great but has a competitor creeping up that no one is paying attention to.」(p.52)
    → **②,答「敘事何時斷」。** 營收放緩但盈利靠削支出化妝,是斷裂的先兆。
13. **紅旗:公司賴空頭。**「When a company blames the price decline in its stock on short sellers, it's a red flag. A company's best revenge against short sellers is simply reporting good numbers.」(p.52)
14. **紅旗:為追熱潮拋棄本業。**「A company that goes from its traditional business to whatever is hot at the time. […] One company we shorted recently went from selling flat panel displays to offering an Internet fax service, trashing their whole business plan in the process.」(p.53)
    → **④最危險的一條紅線。** 用戶四類注買的正是「市場仍在爭論的敘事」,而這一類公司裡有相當比例正是為蹭熱點而拋棄本業的殼。這條紅線可以機械化:申報文本裡主營業務描述的逐年劇變。
15. **紅旗:換核數師、CFO 頻繁更替。**「a change in auditors, can be a major red flag.」Pegasystems 例:「They said they were making the change because their previous accounting firm didn't understand the business and wasn't aggressive enough. But the incredible thing is that people ignored that red flag.」(p.53)
16. **她的完整篩選欄位。**「We also screen for revenue deceleration, earnings deceleration, high P/Es, high inventories, and some technical indicators, such as stocks breaking below their fifty-day moving average.」加上高應收帳(p.53–54)。
17. **技術只用來決定何時不在場。**「When a chart breaks out to a new high, unless I have some really compelling information, I just get out of the way. […] If the stock I am short collapses to support, I will probably get out.」(p.50–51)
    → **與用戶信念②完全同構**:圖表不用來預測,只用來在事件之間決定進出時機。
18. **軋空按組合佔比處理,不按看法。**「Because of the price rise, it had gone up to 7 percent of my portfolio, and I can't allow that.」「If I lose 20 percent on a single stock, I will cover one-third of my position.」(p.51、p.54)
19. **她的優勢來自做過長倉。**「They look at the raw numbers, and they are realists. They don't understand that a lot of people just buy the stock because it's going up or because the chart looks good.」(p.54)純看數字的空頭都被消滅了,因為買家根本不看數字。
    → **③,答「持貨的是誰、是否純資金推動」。**
20. **她自認防守不到的風險源**:她從未做空過互聯網股;Sanchez 一日由 32 升到 80 之後,她開始逐隻檢查組合裡哪一隻會是下一隻「宣布做網上業務」的股。即**純敘事轉向是她的方法無法防守的**。

### 三、Mark Minervini(Quantech)——二類注的極端版本

**他是誰**:初中輟學的前鼓手。1994 年中至 1999 年底單一帳戶年均複合 220%,最差一年 128%,1997 年美國投資錦標賽冠軍。

21. **一句策略。**「Expose your portfolio to the best stocks the market has to offer and cut your losses very quickly when you're wrong.」(p.93)
22. **低價股沒有例外。**「Stocks that are low are usually low for a reason. Typically, the stocks I buy are $20 or higher, and I never buy stocks under $12.」(p.93)超過八成持股的公司成立不足十年。
23. **大贏家在起飛前 P/E 已經高於平均。**「avoiding stocks just because the P/E seems 'too high' will result in missing out on some of the best market moves.」(p.94)
    → **②,直接答「定價已高但增長仍在上調時是否可買」——必須買。**
24. **止蝕才是主戰場。**「Containing your losses is 90 percent of the battle, regardless of the strategy.」勝率只有五成:「What counts isn't how often you're right, but how much you profit on your winning trades versus how much you lose on your losing trades.」(p.94–95)
25. **他回溯自己的紀錄得出封頂止蝕的量化效益。**「I checked what would have happened if I had capped all my losses at 10 percent. […] that simple rule would have increased my profits by 70 percent!」而且「Capping my losses at 10 percent only knocked out a few of my winners. I noticed that the winning trades usually worked from the onset.」(p.97–98)
    → **跨類。** 贏注一開始就贏——這對用戶四類注的「時間止損」候選是實證支持。
26. **止蝕寬度是預期回報的函數。**「The essential principle is that the stop-loss point should be a function of the expected gain.」(p.98)
27. **價格可以否決基本面,基本面不可以否決價格。**「I would never bet on my fundamental ideas without some confirming price action, I might consider buying a stock with apparent negative fundamentals if its relative price performance is in the top 2 percent of the market. […] the price action may be telling you that the stock is discounting a potential change in the fundamentals that is not yet evident. The combination of strong price action and weak current fundamentals often occurs in turnaround companies or companies with a new technology whose potential is not yet widely understood.」(p.98)
    → **這一條直接壓在④上,而且是對用戶信念①與②最強的反例。** 他描述的「基本面差、新技術潛力未被廣泛理解」正正是用戶四類注的定義,但他的觸發鍵是相對強度前 2%,不是敘事研究。
28. **同一形態在牛市早段與末段意思相反。**「When do you get breakouts that fail? In the latter stages of a bull market after the stock has already run up dramatically. Chart patterns are only useful if you know when to apply them; otherwise, you might as well be throwing darts.」(p.99)
29. **怎樣知道自己錯了。**「The stock goes down. That's all you need to know.」(p.101)被震走之後必須有回場計劃(p.100)。

### 四、Steve Cohen(S.A.C.)

**他是誰**:七年年均複合淨回報 45%(收 50% 表現費,即毛利年均約 90%),只有三個虧損月,最差一個月 -2%。

30. **價格變動的歸因分解。**「40 percent of a stock's price movement was due to the market, 30 percent to the sector, and only 30 percent to the stock itself, which is something that I believe is true.」(p.147)
    → **跨類,對「單押個股敘事」的定量警告。** 個股研究只解釋三成;板塊(即用戶的鏈層)解釋另外三成。
31. **催化劑是否仍然成立,是判錯的第一問。**「If I am in the trade because of a catalyst, the first thing I check is whether the catalyst still applies.」(p.147)IBM 例:因預期業績差而做空,業績大好,即晚盤後 $187 補倉,翌日開 $197。
    → **業績事件的處理範式:事件與假設相反,不留過夜。**
32. **勝率。**「My best trader makes money only 63 percent of the time. Most traders make money only in the 50 to 55 percent range.」(p.147)
33. **「太高了」不是理由。**「They short stocks because they are up, as if that were a reason. They'll say, 'I can't believe the stock is so high,' and that's their total research.」(p.148)
    → **③,答「派對何時散」——估值高本身不構成理由,要有具體事件。**
34. **三種進場理由並列。**「Sometimes I trade off the tape—the individual stock price action; sometimes I trade off the sector; and sometimes I trade based on a catalyst. […] The basic idea is that you trade your theory and then let the market tell you whether you are right.」(p.150)
    → **對「事件之間的價格資訊」最直接的表態:報價帶是與催化劑並列的獨立進場理由,不是附屬品。**
35. **指數納入的機械買盤是可計時的事件。**「this stock was going up because it was being added to the S&P index. I figured that once the index fund buying was completed, the stock would sell off.」(p.150)
    → **③,直接支持登記表丁族「可持有門檻跨越」候選,並補上它的另一半:買盤完結那一刻也是可算的。**
36. **他判斷狂熱終局用參與者廣度,不用估值。**「Everybody in the world is talking stocks now. Everybody wants to be a trader. To me that is the sign of something ending, not something beginning. You can't have everybody on one side of the fence.」(p.150)
37. **事件前預掛遠離市價的盤。** 聯儲局公佈前十五分鐘他掛滿遠價買賣盤,「In case the market does something stupid」(p.146)。

### 五、Stuart Walton(Reindeer Capital)

**他是誰**:三藩市單人操盤,1991–1999 年均複合毛利 115%,最好一年 274%、最差 63%;管理 1.5 億美元時仍然只得自己一個。持股平均只有數星期。

38. **方法組成:基本面只佔四分之一。**「The fundamentals of the stock are only about 25 percent of it. […] Another 25 percent is technical. […] Another 25 percent is watching how a stock responds to different information: macroeconomic events, its own news flow. […] The last 25 percent is my gut feeling for the direction of the market as a whole.」(p.15–16)
39. **買被祝福的,不買便宜的。**「Good companies, on balance, continue to go up. […] I look for companies that have been blessed by the market. […] no matter how many brokers tell their clients to buy these other stocks because they are cheap.」(p.15)
    → **②的正面表述,同時是對①的直接否定。**
40. **看股票怎樣消化消息。**「I want to see a stock move higher on good news […] and not give much ground on negative news. If the stock responds poorly to negative news then it hasn't been blessed.」(p.16)
    → **與 Little Book 的「消息反應失配」是同一件事。**
41. **「有夢無盈利」的 IPO 令他破功。**「The market was getting very excited about conceptual IPOs—stocks with a dream and a story but no earnings. When stocks like these go sour, they can go down 70 percent or more very quickly. It was as if a tornado had swept through my portfolio.」(p.20)
    → **③④的下檔形狀:七成以上、極快。** 他的處理是全部清倉,理由是資金留在死股上等反彈不如換去會升的股(p.20)。
42. **管理層不能信,除非股價已死。**「either they told me what they had previously told everyone else, and it was already factored into the price, or else they lied to me.」但「I might call a company's management when its stock is very low and no one is talking to them, because that is when they are usually desperate enough to talk to anyone.」(p.20–21)
43. **貼在報價機上的規則(節錄)**:「Prices move before fundamentals.」「It is a warning flag if the market is not responding to data correctly.」「Force yourself to trade against the consensus.」(p.21)

## ② 跨受訪者共通點與盲點

**共通點(這五位一致)**
- **催化劑是必需品,不是加分項。** Lauer、Galante、Cohen 三人各自獨立地說:估值(高或低)本身不構成行動理由,一定要有一件可計時的事。這是全書最一致、也最貼近用戶四類注的一條。
- **市場對消息的反應,凌駕消息本身。** Walton(p.16)、Lauer(p.28)、Cohen(p.147)、Schwager 總結第 21 條(p.162)全部講同一件事:你預期的消息出了而市場不照你想的反應,那就是你錯了的證據。
- **勝率五成上下,靠賺賠比。** Minervini 50%、Cohen 55%、Cohen 最好的手下 63%。
- **技術的用途被統一收窄到「時機與確認」,不是預測。** Galante 用來決定何時不在場;Minervini 用來確認基本面;Walton 佔四分之一權重;Cohen 讀報價帶的量。**這正正是用戶信念②的內容**,而且是四個獨立來源給出的同一收窄。

**盲點三個**
- **時代與品種偏差。** 五位全部是 1990 年代美股,而且四位有做空能力。用戶是不能做空、不能用槓桿的散戶,Galante 那一章的可轉移部分只有紅線清單,不是策略。
- **生存者偏差。** Lauer 訪問後數年,他的 Lancer 基金因為對非流通持股估值造假而崩塌並涉及刑事——書中他那套「集中在無人問津的小型股」正是造假的溫床。**這一點書裡不可能有,但用戶必須知道:第 1、7 條那種極度集中在流動性極差的錯殺股的做法,連專業人士都守不住估值紀律。**
- **沒有一位量度「共識」。** 五人全部靠直覺判斷「市場知不知道」(Cohen 的「人人都在談股票」、Walton 的「逆共識」)。用戶框架的第二條軸在這本書裡沒有工具,只有語感——這反過來說明登記表乙族(敘事共識度量)是原創空間。

## ③ 與用戶框架的衝突處(直說)

1. **Walton 與 Minervini 直接否定一類注。** 「Stocks that are low are usually low for a reason」(p.93)、「Good companies, on balance, continue to go up […] no matter how many brokers tell their clients to buy these other stocks because they are cheap」(p.15)。兩位最高回報率的受訪者都明言不買便宜貨。用戶的一類注要成立,不能靠「基本面仍強而已跌」,必須靠 Lauer 那套六道閘——尤其是第 5 道(可計時的催化劑)。**沒有催化劑的一類注,在這本書的證據下是負期望。**
2. **Minervini 第 27 條直接挑戰用戶信念①與②。** 他說相對強度前 2% 可以推翻明顯差的基本面,理由是價格正在折現一個尚未顯現的基本面轉變。這既否定了「基本面是核心」(價格可以先於基本面),也否定了「技術長期無用」(他的持倉期以月計、方法用了十幾年)。用戶可以不同意,但要寫得出為什麼——不能當它不存在。
3. **Lauer 的「集中即優勢」與用戶「注內集中」同向,但風險被低估。** Lauer 說分散是「對自己持股無知的對沖」,而他本人後來正是死在集中加流動性不足。用戶的「相信 CPO 就押一兩隻」在方向上有支持,但**支持它的人示範了它的死法**。
4. **回報預期的落差。** 用戶估一類注 10–20%;Lauer 的一類注目標是十二個月翻一倍,而他的門檻是「大市上升時急插五成以上」。這說明一類注不是一條光譜,是兩種東西:輕度低估(10–20%)與災難後的錯殺(翻倍),後者的樣本量少得多、要求高得多。用戶的表應該分開這兩格。
5. **不衝突但要補的一格:不落注。** 五位全部大量時間在等,Galante 更明言三條件不全就不做。用戶的四類注表沒有「不落注」這一類。

## ⑤ 一句判斷

這本書給了用戶一類注一套現成的、可機械化的六道閘(Lauer),給了四類注一張可由申報文本偵測的紅旗清單(Galante),但同時由最高回報者 Walton 明言「不再買便宜貨、賣升得多的股,改為買貴的股」(p.15,原書無「買便宜貨是輸家做法」逐字原句),Minervini 補一句「相對強度前 2% 可以推翻明顯差的基本面」(p.98)——**用戶框架裡最舒服的那一類(一類注)在這本書裡是最沒有支持的一類,而最沒有把握的那一類(四類注),書裡的人是靠價格而不是靠敘事研究找到的。**
